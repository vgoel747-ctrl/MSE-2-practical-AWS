Pet Care Guidelines

1. Proper Nutrition

Provide age-appropriate food and fresh water at all times.

2. Regular Veterinary Checkups

Keep up with annual vaccinations and routine preventive care.

3. Exercise & Mental Stimulation

Ensure daily play time, walks, and social interactions for physical health.
